# 三种配色变体：实际生图提示词

三次均使用内置 imagegen，独立编辑同一张 04 配色稿；两张原始美术标杆仅提供风格与色彩参考。程序不负责重绘或换色。以下为三次实际模型提示词，英文保留为工具输入。

```text

Do NOT add, remove, rearrange or resize objects. This is one independent color variation of the SAME SOURCE, not a new scene, new layout, or a cumulative edit of another variant. Keep all existing content, including the lighthouse/car, rooftop radio, ocean telephone booth, "不含海景" note and original headlines. Do not insert an A/B/C label, palette swatches or explanatory labels into the game image.

COMMON COLOR PRINCIPLES: very dark ocean/backdrop; distinctly colored but restrained midtone continents and colored paper backings; clean fresh warm off-white writing surfaces; near-black ink; one clearly stronger warm ochre/mustard investigation CTA. Color belongs to separate objects, NOT a global filter over everything. Paper faces remain the same clean warm off-white family in every colorway, never tinted green, blue, pink or sepia. All writing stays high-contrast. Keep broad flat painted low-poly facets, coarse hand-drawn accessories and wry editorial personality. All variants must feel related to the two art benchmarks, contemporary and tactile, not ancient maps, military radar, photography, sterile vector UI, crumpled paper, dirty noise or new tiny detail. No neon, electric royal blue, bright white ocean, washed-out pastel background, muddy global gray, global dimming or added vignette. Preserve hand-painted finish rather than applying a simple uniform color overlay.

Keep the current local light/dark structure in the three news illustrations; harmonize their color planes gently with the new palette WITHOUT changing imagery or repainting them into more realistic detail. The sea around the booth, the dawn radio sky and lighthouse dusk can retain distinct local moods. Keep the lamp glow warm and restricted, not bloom.

Produce ONE finished full-screen LANDSCAPE 16:9 recolored image, matching the source framing. Palette variant for THIS output follows:
A: INK TEAL / MINERAL TEAL / HONEY OCHRE. A livelier blue-green editorial palette drawn from the benchmarks' teal media and blue-green painted scenes. Backdrop is very dark blue-black with a restrained petrol-teal undertone. Continents use clear medium mineral teal, smoky cyan and a smaller set of slate-blue planes; keep enough colored pigment to avoid the source's cold-gray wash. Olive appears only sparingly, never yellow-green masses. The existing blue backing behind the radio stays restrained muted cornflower blue, and the existing backing under the footer becomes richer petroleum teal. Tape remains quiet sage/cream. Proof-strip terracotta is a tiny warm counterpoint. CTA is warm honey ochre, not orange-red. The result should feel curious, fresh, quietly nocturnal, with visible lively midtones rather than military green.
```
